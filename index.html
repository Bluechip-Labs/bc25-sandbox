Achievements

Mint BC25 · primary market, at NAV

You deposit test USDC and receive BC25 shares priced at NAV. A 1% entry fee goes to the protocol treasury.

Redeem BC25 · back to USDC

Burn BC25 shares for their NAV value in USDC. A 2% exit fee applies.

Swap on the DEX · secondary market

On a real exchange you buy BC25 from other holders — no protocol fee, just a small market spread. Arbitragers keep the market price near NAV. (This is the "two-market" model: mint at NAV with a fee, or buy on the DEX at the market price.)

Send BC25 · the token moves freely

BC25 is a normal transferable ERC token — send it to a friend's wallet. (Simulated.)
